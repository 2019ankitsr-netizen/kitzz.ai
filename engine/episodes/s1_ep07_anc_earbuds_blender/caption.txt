Your earbuds make noise to give you silence. 🎧

Here's how noise cancelling works:
🎤 A tiny mic on the outside listens to the sound around you, like an engine hum
🔄 A chip flips that sound wave upside down, so every peak becomes a dip
➕ Play both together and they cancel out: peak + dip = flat = silence
⚡ It redoes this thousands of times a second. Even a tiny delay and noise leaks through
👂 A second mic inside your ear checks what's left and fixes it
✈️ It works best on low, steady hums (planes, buses, ACs). High, sharp sounds like voices are mostly blocked by the silicone ear tip
📜 The idea was patented in 1936, and decades later it went into headsets for pilots
