Every banana you eat is radioactive. 🍌☢️

Here's the science:
⚛️ Bananas are full of potassium, and a tiny bit of natural potassium is radioactive: potassium-40
📟 One banana gives you about 0.1 microsieverts
🦷 A dental X-ray is roughly 50 bananas' worth
✅ It doesn't build up: your body keeps its potassium steady and flushes out the extra
📏 Scientists even joke about it as a unit: the Banana Equivalent Dose
