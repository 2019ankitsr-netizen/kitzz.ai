Honey can last 3,000 years. 🍯⏳

Here's why it never spoils:
🏺 Archaeologists found pots of honey in ancient Egyptian tombs, still edible
💧 Honey is only about 17% water, so germs that land in it dry out
🍊 It's acidic, roughly as sour as orange juice (pH ≈ 3.9)
🐝 Bees add an enzyme that slowly makes hydrogen peroxide, a built-in germ killer
🫙 Hard and cloudy? It's just crystallised, not spoiled. Warm the jar in hot water
🔒 Keep the lid shut: honey pulls water from the air
