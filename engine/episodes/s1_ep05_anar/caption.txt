An anar is basically a rocket that's not allowed to take off. 🎆

Here's what's happening inside that little clay pot:
🧱 Tightly packed powder: fuel, plus a chemical that carries its own oxygen
🔥 It burns from the top down, layer by layer, making a rush of hot gas
⬆️ The only way out is the tiny hole, so the gas shoots up as a jet and pushes the pot into the ground
✨ The sparks are tiny bits of metal like iron and aluminium, glowing white hot
🎨 The colours are metal salts: strontium = red, barium = green, copper = blue
💥 Trap the same gas with no way out, and you get a bang instead
