Your internet isn't in the cloud. It's lying on the ocean floor. 🌊

Hit play on a video and here's the trip it takes:
📱 Phone → nearest tower (the only wireless hop)
💡 Tower → underground glass fibre, as pulses of light
🏖️ Coast → a cable landing station, then straight into the sea
🌍 Cables about as thick as a garden hose cross entire oceans
🖥️ A data centre on the far shore sends your video all the way back

Around 99% of data between continents travels this way. Satellites carry under 1%.
