Your microwave never heats your food. Your food heats itself. ☕

Here's how a microwave actually works:
💧 Food is full of water, and every water molecule has a plus end and a minus end, like a tiny magnet
📡 A part called a magnetron fires invisible microwaves
🔄 Their electric field flips 2.45 billion times a second, so the water molecules keep twisting to line up
🔥 All that twisting jostles the molecules around them, and that jostling is heat
🍽️ The waves leave hot and cold spots, which is why the plate spins
⚡ Metal bounces the waves back, and sharp edges can spark. Keep the spoon out
🍫 In 1945, engineer Percy Spencer stood next to a radar magnetron and the chocolate bar in his pocket melted
