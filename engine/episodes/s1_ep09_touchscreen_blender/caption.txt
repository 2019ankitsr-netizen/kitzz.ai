Your phone can't feel your finger. It feels your electricity. 📱

Here's how a touchscreen actually works:
🔲 Under the glass is a grid of see-through wires, rows one way and columns the other
⚡ Every crossing point holds a tiny electric charge
👆 Your body conducts electricity, so your finger pulls a little charge away from the crossings under it
🔍 A chip checks every crossing over a hundred times a second and finds where the charge dropped. That's your tap
↔️ Move your finger and the dip moves too. Two dips? Pinch to zoom
🧤 That's why normal gloves don't work. But a sausage does, because it conducts just like you
📜 This kind of touchscreen was invented in England in 1965 and first used for air traffic control
